How to Play

⌨️ Mouse & Keyboard

  • WASD move · Mouse look (click to lock)
  • Your primary attack fires automatically at the nearest enemy (hold Left Click to fire at the crosshair when nothing is in range)
  • Right Click / Q Ability 1
  • Shift Ability 2
  • R Ultimate
  • Space / Mouse wheel up jump (multi-jump!) — jump right as you land to bunny hop
  • E / F interact (chests, portal)
  • Tab hold for items & stats · Esc pause
  • 1 / 2 / 3 pick a level-up

🎮 Controller

  • Left stick move · Right stick look
  • Primary attack is automatic · LT Ability 1
  • RB Ability 2 · Y / LB Ultimate
  • A jump · X interact
  • View/Back items & stats · Start pause
  • Menus: D-pad/stick + A, B back

📱 Touch

  • Left side: virtual joystick
  • Right side: drag to look
  • Your primary attack fires automatically
  • Buttons for abilities, ultimate, jump, interact
  • shows items & stats

🗺️ The Loop

  • Kill enemies for coins and XP. Level-ups offer tomes (max 4 per run, up to Lv 99), weapons (your main weapon + up to 3 more, up to Lv 50), or both. Rarer rolls give more.
  • Spend coins on chests: Common · Rare · Epic · Legendary.
  • Find the portal , interact to summon the boss.
  • Stay in the ring to charge the portal, kill the boss, then leave.
  • Difficulty rises exponentially with time and stages. Don't dawdle.
  • Achievements unlock new survivors and items. Every unlock can be earned from a fresh save.
